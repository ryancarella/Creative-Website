# Carella Kitchen
Marketing venture for a planned macro-conscious food truck in Fairfield.
Existing React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind v4 and Framer Motion project.
Live: https://ryancarella.github.io/Creative-Website/
Interactive menu filters and portion planner. Bowl ideas are saved only during the
current visit, with no orders, payments, or personal information collected.
The menu/prices page is at https://ryancarella.github.io/Creative-Website/#pricing .
Selected standard-item prices: chicken/rice bowl $14, ground beef bowl $15,
pasta $13, protein bar $3.50, protein shake $6, all USD per item.
Recipes, nutrition, portions, launch date, and schedule remain unverified.
No custom-portion, extra-protein, bundle, checkout or payment pricing is offered.
The pricing page supports category filters and comparison of up to three options.
Homepage prices and the pricing page share the public-only menu data module.
Private planning records are kept outside this repository and are not bundled.
